# Choose Your Own Adventure

A console adventure in which a player prepares for an encounter with a dragon.

## Language

**Merchant**:
The trader encountered on the southern path who offers magical armor and a weapon enchantment.

**Gold**:
The player's currency for merchant purchases.

**Weapon enchantment**:
A magical improvement to the player's existing weapon that adds a fixed damage bonus to successful, undefended hits.
_Avoid_: Replacement weapon

**Magical armor**:
Protective equipment that reduces incoming damage to the player.
